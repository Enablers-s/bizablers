Company setup

Three ways in. We help you choose the right one.

The right structure depends on where you will trade, who you will sell to and how you plan to grow. We map it out before anything is filed.

01

Mainland

Trade directly across the local market, work with government and take on any commercial activity your licence allows.

  • Trade licence & registration
  • Municipality & government approvals
  • Office and Ejari support
02

Free zone

Set up quickly in a dedicated zone, with streamlined licensing and a wide choice of locations and packages.

  • Zone selection & licence
  • Flexi-desk to full office
  • Fast, hassle-free process
03

International

Structure across borders: holding companies, branches and subsidiaries in the markets you are expanding into.

  • International structuring
  • Branch & subsidiary setup
  • Multi-country expansion
